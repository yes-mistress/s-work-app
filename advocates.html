Worker-led.

The essential infrastructure for sex worker rights.

The tools that sex workers rely on to run their businesses were built by people who would prefer they didn't exist. You can feel it in every product decision, and workers pay for it in time, safety, and income.

s—work is sex worker owned and operated. We are building the tool that should have existed long before now: a secure, private inbox and client management platform where a worker's safety information, booking history, and communications live in one encrypted place that cannot be taken away by a platform ban.

Interested in partnering with us? Contact the s—work team →
●

How we help workers be safer

Dangerous client flags are append-only: once a record exists it cannot be silently deleted or edited, only added to. We maintain a shared blacklist within the platform and are open to working with other blacklist holders to connect that information across communities.

The built-in check-in system lets a worker set a session timer with a trusted contact. If they haven't checked out when the session ends, that contact is automatically alerted.

●

How we help workers manage their business

Sex work is a business. Workers deserve to see where their income is coming from, understand what is and isn't working, and make decisions based on their own data rather than guesswork.

If you want to maximise what you earn, that is as legitimate a goal as any other and we are building for it. If you want to work less without earning less, same thing — the data helps with that too.

●

How we work as a community

A worker who spends less time managing fragmented inboxes, whose screening history cannot be destroyed by a platform ban, who controls their own data and uses it to run a better business, has more time and energy for the political and legal work that protects the whole community.

Worker-controlled digital infrastructure is a small piece of that, and we intend to build it well.
